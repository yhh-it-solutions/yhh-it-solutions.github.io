Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes our policies and procedures on the collection, use, and disclosure of your personal information when you use Hamro Points (the "App" or "Service") and outlines your privacy rights and how applicable laws protect you.

Hamro Points is a reward and loyalty platform developed and operated by YHH IT Solutions Pvt. Ltd. ("the Company", "we", "us", or "our"), located in Pokhara, Nepal. YHH IT Solutions Pvt. Ltd. acts as the data controller for your information.

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

For the purposes of this Privacy Policy:

  • Account means a unique account created for you to access our Service or specific features of our Service.
  • Application (or "App") refers to Hamro Points, the mobile application software provided by the Company on Android and iOS.
  • Company refers to YHH IT Solutions Pvt. Ltd., Pokhara, Nepal.
  • Personal Data means any information that relates to an identified or identifiable individual.
  • Business Partners / Participating Merchants refers to third-party commercial partners, stores, and businesses offering reward discounts, points-earning opportunities, or freebies through the Service.
  • Service Provider means any natural or legal person who processes data on behalf of the Company (such as cloud hosting, analytics, and notification providers).
  • Usage Data refers to data collected automatically, generated either by the use of the Service or from the Service infrastructure itself (e.g., duration of a screen visit).
  • You means the individual accessing or using the Service.

Information We Collect

We collect information to provide, maintain, and optimize your reward experience. We collect only the information described below:

1. Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and phone number when registering or managing your profile.
  • Points & Rewards Activity: Your accumulated points balance, records of points earned, redemption history, discount coupons generated, and claimed freebies.

2. Information Collected with Your Permission while Using the Application

To deliver specific features, we may collect the following with your prior consent:

  • Location Data: Approximate or precise GPS location to display nearby participating merchant outlets, localized deals, discounts, and point-earning locations. You can enable or disable location permissions at any time through your device settings.
  • Camera and Photo Library: To scan partner merchant QR codes for earning or redeeming points and discounts, or to upload receipts/profile pictures where supported.

3. Information Collected Automatically (Usage Data)

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, language, and mobile network information.
  • Usage Details: In-app interactions, features accessed, screens viewed, time spent, and referral sources.
  • Diagnostics: Crash logs and system performance metrics to diagnose technical bugs.
  • IP Address: Used for general location detection and security fraud prevention.

4. Third-Party Sign-In Providers

If you choose to sign in using a third-party authentication provider (such as Google or Apple), we receive basic profile details (such as your name and verified email address) from that provider.

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the Service: Operating Hamro Points, maintaining your points balance, and tracking your tier status.
  • To manage your Account: Registering and authenticating your user profile and securing access to in-app features.
  • To process Rewards and Redemptions: Issuing discount vouchers, processing freebie redemptions, and verifying claims with participating partner merchants.
  • To contact You: Sending you service notifications, security alerts, and transaction receipts via email, SMS, or mobile push notifications.
  • To provide Special Offers and Updates: Sending news, promotional campaigns, special point multipliers, and discount alerts related to our partners (which you can opt out of at any time).
  • To detect and prevent Fraud: Monitoring points manipulation, automated bot activity, duplicate redemptions, and security incidents.
  • To improve our Service: Analyzing user trends to enhance user experience, optimize app performance, and expand merchant partnerships.
  • To comply with Legal Obligations: Satisfying applicable accounting, tax, and regulatory requirements.

Legal Bases for Processing (EEA / UK users): If you reside in the EEA or UK, we process your personal data under the following legal grounds under the GDPR:

  • Contract Performance: To fulfill our agreement to provide Hamro Points and manage your loyalty account.
  • Consent: For push notifications, camera/photo access, precise location tracking, and marketing communications (which you can withdraw at any time).
  • Legitimate Interests: To keep our platform secure, prevent points fraud, and improve our services.
  • Legal Obligation: To comply with statutory obligations.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Hamro Points uses cookies, software development kits (SDKs), and similar mobile identifiers to operate and improve the Service:

  • Essential: Required for account authentication, session management, and security.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics and Google Firebase help us understand feature utilization and improve stability.

You can reset or restrict your advertising identifier in your mobile device settings at any time.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share information only in the following specific circumstances:

  • With Participating Merchants & Business Partners: When you redeem points for a discount voucher or freebie, we share necessary redemption details (such as a voucher code or verification token) with the fulfilling merchant so they can validate and honor the offer.
  • With Service Providers: Trusted third-party vendors who assist with infrastructure, hosting, analytics, and notification delivery (including Google Firebase and Google Analytics). These providers are contractually bound to process data solely on our behalf.
  • For Business Transfers: In connection with or during negotiations of any merger, sale of company assets, financing, or acquisition of our business.
  • Legal and Safety: When required by law, subpoena, or government authority, or to protect the safety, rights, and property of YHH IT Solutions Pvt. Ltd., our users, or the public.
  • With Your Consent: For any other purpose disclosed to you with your prior explicit consent.

Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy:

  • Account & Points Data: Retained while your account remains active. Upon account deletion, personal profile details are deleted within 30 to 90 days, except where financial, tax, or legal records must be preserved.
  • Usage and Analytics Data: Retained for a limited duration (typically 14 to 26 months), after which it is aggregated or anonymized.
  • Diagnostic & Crash Logs: Retained for a limited period for troubleshooting and then automatically discarded.

International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your country of residence, including cloud servers operated by providers such as Google in the United States and other international regions. Where required by applicable law, we implement standard safeguards such as EU Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent legal mechanisms to safeguard transferred data.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures, including HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit, role-based access restrictions, and password hashing. However, no electronic transmission over the internet or storage system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a data breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and relevant authorities as required by law.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access & Portability: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete details.
  • Deletion: Request erasure of your personal data.
  • Withdraw Consent: Revoke permissions (location, notifications, camera) at any time via device settings.

To exercise your rights, email us at contact@yhhits.com with the subject line "Privacy Request". We respond within 30 days without penalty or discrimination.

If you are located in the EEA or UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the CCPA/CPRA to know, access, delete, and correct your personal information, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal data (which we do not engage in).

Account and Data Deletion

You can delete your account and associated personal data at any time through either of the following methods:

  • In the App: Navigate to Settings > Account > Delete Account and follow the on-screen prompts.
  • By Email: Contact us at contact@yhhits.com with the subject "Delete my data" from your registered email address.

Upon account deletion, your profile information and active points balance will be deleted within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.
